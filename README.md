# Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

The single sentence a house cat would say in its own defence in the five seconds after knocking a glass off the table: the glass was a traitor working for the floor, and the cat merely exposed it before it could leak the location of the good sunbeam. No apology, no confession — just a cat with a theory and total confidence.
